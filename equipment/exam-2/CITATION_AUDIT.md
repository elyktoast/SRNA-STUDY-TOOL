These citations are NOT proof that every question, numerical answer, explanation, slide image, or clinical claim has been individually confirmed in the cited chapter. No page numbers or verbatim textbook quotations are asserted. Items should be individually checked against chapter text before being labeled source-verified. Questions requiring primary-source validation remain a separate editorial task.
